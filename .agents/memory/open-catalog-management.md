---
name: Open catalog management
description: Owner's explicit choice to remove the catalog admin boundary.
---

Catalog management is intentionally public. Do not reintroduce an admin-key gate by default for adding, editing, deleting, importing, or viewing inventory fields.

**Why:** The owner explicitly said they do not have an admin key and asked to make everything open after being told that visitors could change pricing and inventory. This supersedes the earlier distinction between public submissions and private management.

**How to apply:** Keep catalog changes accessible without credentials unless the owner requests access controls later. Retain validation and sensible abuse limits; openness does not require accepting malformed data or unlimited requests.
